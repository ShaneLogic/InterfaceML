Algorithm Overview

InterfaceML uses two generative model architectures for fullerene structure prediction: an EGNN with denoising diffusion (DDPM/DDIM) and a PaiNN with flow matching. Both are SE(3)-equivariant, meaning they respect rotational and translational symmetry. Below are the key workflows and equations.

Key Equations

EGNN Coordinate Update

$$\Delta x_i = \sum_j \alpha_{ij}(x_i - x_j), \quad \alpha_{ij} = \tanh(\text{MLP}(m_{ij})) \cdot e^{-d^2/2}$$

PaiNN Scalar-Vector Interaction

$$\Delta s = a_{ss} + a_{sv} \cdot \langle U_v, V_v \rangle$$

Inner product encodes bond angles between vector channels.

DDPM Forward Process

$$q(x_t|x_0) = \mathcal{N}\!\left(\sqrt{\bar\alpha_t}\, x_0,\; (1-\bar\alpha_t)I\right)$$

Flow Matching

$$x_t = (1-t)x_0 + t\xi, \quad v = \xi - x_0$$

Time-Weighted Physics Loss

$$\mathcal{L} = \sum_i \lambda_i \cdot w(t) \cdot \mathcal{L}_i, \quad w(t) = \sigma(-15(t/T - 0.4))$$
